# Equivalence of Distance-Based and RKHS-Based Statistics in Hypothesis Testing

Section 5.1, Theorem 22 relates population energy to squared kernel MMD.
STATADIC proves diagonal cancellation for integrable symmetric kernels on arbitrary
probability laws. For a supplied Hilbert feature map with integrable first and
second moments, it also proves equality to twice the squared distance between
the Bochner means. The finite identities remain available for empirical laws.

A zero result therefore identifies equal feature means. To identify equal
laws, the mean embedding must distinguish laws on the chosen moment class.
STATADIC-MMD-007 proves the equivalence between this injectivity condition and
separation by the population squared MMD of equation (3.3).
Section 6, Proposition 29 connects this additional condition to strong negative
