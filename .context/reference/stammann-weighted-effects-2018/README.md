# Fast and Feasible Estimation of Generalized Linear Models with High-Dimensional k-way Fixed Effects

The third revision, p. 6, equation (3.4), writes the one-factor residual projection in whitened coordinates. Substituting a whitened response and dividing out its row scale gives the ordinary weighted within-group residual. The finite-group sum-of-squares calculation in STATADIC-PANEL-001 proves the minimizing property directly. The paper treats repeated projections and GLM iteration separately.
