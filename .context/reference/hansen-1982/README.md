# Large Sample Properties of Generalized Method of Moments Estimators

Hansen introduces the sample GMM criterion before Definition 2.1 on p. 1034.
For affine sample moments with a fixed weight factor and nonsingular weighted
Gram matrix, STATADIC derives the exact objective gap from the normal equations.
Hansen’s large-sample consistency and covariance results have additional
assumptions and remain open in the registry. [Author copy](https://larspeterhansen.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Hansen-econometrica-GMM.pdf) · [Source mapping](source.json)
