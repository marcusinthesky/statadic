# Aitken Equations

Wiley's [publisher record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/0471667196.ess0249.pub2)
identifies the author, publication date, and DOI. The [publisher-hosted text](https://catalogimages.wiley.com/images/db/pdf/0471743917.01.pdf),
pp. 103–104, equations (3)–(4) and the following paragraph, states the
known positive-definite covariance model, weighted normal equations, weighted
residual minimization, and minimum-variance linear unbiased contrast. STATADIC specializes the covariance to known positive diagonal precisions.
It proves unbiasedness and the variance ordering for every linear unbiased
contrast on general square-integrable laws with an invertible weighted Gram
matrix. The earlier finite-law proof works in whitened coordinates.
The online entry appeared in August 2006 and the print edition in December 2005.
