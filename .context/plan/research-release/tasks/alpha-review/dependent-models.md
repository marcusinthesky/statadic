## Evidence scope

Twelve catalogue groups contain 69 variant records: 64 mapped, one source-approved
association (already F005), two retained-source campaign statuses and 67 pending.
Five gaps are spatial survival mean and joint-geometry/chronological-bandwidth/
time-block-refit/multi-operator graph procedures. Log-logistic estimation maps only
to unconditional mean bias/MSE, not coefficient/covariance coverage or testing.
Repeated-spell fixed effects have no separately scoped campaign variant.

Panel external references compare seven estimators and seven covariance choices
on balanced/unbalanced weighted designs; their long-run kernels are Bartlett.
Lifelines covers Weibull/lognormal ordinary and repeated-spell fits, not general
log-logistic inference. Existing spatial-survival qualification files are numerical
stress controls, not size/coverage campaigns. Integration information/HVPs need
an independent multi-censor small-case oracle. The 192 composition pilot records
are smoke evidence and preserve adverse seasonal coverage.

Historical retained references include 76 relevant cases, subject to F007. This
review freshly ran the four graph-likelihood reference cells with selected AFT/
survival tests: 37 tests passed. That is bounded current source evidence, not
qualification of learned graphs or every dependent variant.

## Formal/source boundaries

The selected 25 mappings include spatial determinant/observed-covariance/impact/
moment identities, GMM/IV bridges, KKP commutation, survival observation/delayed-
entry/AFT factors, dyadic endpoint rebasing and panel/cluster covariance bridges.
No additional direct Lean contradiction was established. Their hypotheses do
not prove missingness ignorability, exogeneity, numerical integration accuracy,
graph identification, solver correctness or joint-geometry uncertainty.

KKP's equation and appendix exposed F024 despite an external software comparator.
Log-logistic has a generic Jacobian-chain-rule link; it lacks the equivalent
normalized-baseline specialization established for Weibull/lognormal. Refinement
compares independent fixed-draw validations; its stability is not an error bound.
All human source reviews remain pending.
