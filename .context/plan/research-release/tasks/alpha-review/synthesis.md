## Cohesion decisions

1. Keep Equation for structural composition and Conditional for bounded laws,
   with intentional OLS/WLS/PanelOLS presets. Migrate downstream consumers; do
   not restore the universal Regression composer or old GLM constructors.
2. Keep preparation, native fitting, inference, reporting and frozen artifacts
   separate. Reports must not quietly refit. Estimators cannot override declared
   dispersion. Share pure live/frozen prediction numerics; keep real wire codecs.
3. Freeze all prepared state, including Arrow extras and atom probabilities.
   Checked operations must preserve the truth of evidence as well as metadata.
4. Keep explicit availability/capabilities. Finite fits do not imply calibrated
   tests, eligible J statistics, separating energy or general barycentre
   convergence. Narrow or reject unsupported operations.
5. Keep actual-W/preliminary/final-meat, native QR/generic, signed-estimate/metric
   and objective/fitted-solution distinctions. Remove duplicate wrappers and
   false selectors; preserve independently usable documented utilities.
6. Keep native PlotSpec/PGFplots and optional publishing. Use one vendored TeX
   resource owner. Correct manuscript migrations/freshness before qualification.
7. Keep source, Lean, independent reference, Monte Carlo and execution evidence
   separate. All 153 principal claim bodies were inspected in mathematical scope;
   fresh compilation, full correspondence and human fidelity approval remain
   separate. Exact links do not spread evidence across a facade.

## Proposed alpha boundaries

Retain linear estimation/covariance, bounded conditional laws, prepared inputs,
basic discrepancies/transport and native extension contracts as the central
workflow, subject to their corrective gates. Shared ownership, invalid-lane and
import defects currently prevent declaring this workflow ready.

Narrow deletion diagnostics, auxiliary-test rank conditions, efficient-J eligibility,
energy refinement, spectral differentiation and barycentre convergence to justified
operations. Removing an unsupported alpha option is preferable to silently adding
a new calibration or claiming a stronger theorem.

Keep advanced moments, graph learning, spatial/panel/survival/dyadic and optimized
inference explicitly experimental until their sources, failures, calibration and
current provenance meet the prespecified gates. Register omitted hurdle,
positive-Poisson and diagnostic science or defer those qualification claims.
These are editorial recommendations, not production deletions or a claim that
every experimental implementation is wrong. Preserve valid distinctions and
adverse evidence.

## JAX and resource decisions

Use grad, JVP/VJP and Hessian/HVP for named targets with required smoothness.
Smooth covariance/chunked controls passed; repeated-eigenvalue spectral gradients
and a private CUE second derivative do not support broad promises. Translating
both scalar-optimizer bounds and objective tests translation equivariance, not
solution sensitivity at fixed bounds. Rank switches, adaptive termination and
branch choices require explicit limits.

Keep stable compiled callables and explicit array inputs. Host alignment, exact
permutation enumeration and provenance remain outside traced numerical work.
Bounded Gamma logs show repeated compilation. Pure-SLX identity filters, retained
IV derivative tensors and full QP gradients per coordinate have concrete resource
fixes. jit, lax or AD syntax alone does not establish efficient numerical work.

Current speed and memory superiority is unestablished. Historical CPU44 uses
earlier source identities and has mixed outcomes. Require correct matched outputs
first, then compare repeated CPU work, cold/preparation/compilation and total RSS
separately. See [the repair/cut queue](backlog.md) and
[the campaign contract](qualification.md).
