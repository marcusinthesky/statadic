\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

STATADIC provides reusable statistical components on JAX, with explicit contracts
and separate records for the different kinds of evidence a method can have. Its
fitting workflows are conventional; its formal, validation and publication
infrastructure makes their assumptions and limits inspectable. As statistical
implementations become easier to generate and optimize, preserving what a
computation means, from mathematics through code to a published result, becomes
correspondingly more important.
